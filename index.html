What it does

Duplicates

Find repeated rows by any columns. Highlight, label, move or delete them. Ignores case and stray spaces.

Compare sheets

Which leads are already customers? Which emails are missing? Mark, copy or remove the rows.

Clean text

Trim spaces, remove hidden characters and line breaks, fix quotes, strip HTML, change case.

Split

Split full names into title, first, middle, last and suffix, or split any column by a separator.

Merge

Bring columns in from another sheet by a key. Like VLOOKUP, without formulas to break.

Fix numbers

Turn "1,234.50", "$12" or "12%" stored as text into real numbers. IDs with leading zeros stay safe.

Help

Open Rinse: in Google Sheets, go to Extensions, then Rinse, then Open Rinse.

Which cells does it use? The cells you select. If you select only one cell, Rinse uses the whole table on that sheet. Tick "First row is headers" if row 1 holds column names.

Undo: press "Undo last" at the top of the sidebar, or Extensions, Rinse, Undo last Rinse change. This restores the whole sheet to how it was before the last Rinse change. Backups are hidden tabs named _rinse_backup_; you can delete them anytime.

Activate Pro: after buying you get a license key by email. In the sidebar, click "I have a key", paste it, and press Activate. To move it to another Google account, click "Remove key" first.

Duplicates: tick the columns that should match (for example Email). Leave all unticked to compare full rows. "Keep the first one" leaves the first copy alone and flags the rest.

Compare and Merge: pick the key columns on both sheets in the same order, for example Email here and Email there.
