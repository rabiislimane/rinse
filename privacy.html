Privacy policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Rinse is made by Pandapi ("we"), which makes the Rinse add-on for Google Sheets. This policy explains what data the add-on uses and why.

Spreadsheet data

Rinse asks Google for permission to view and manage only the spreadsheet in which you open it, and to show its sidebar. It reads the cells you choose so it can find duplicates, clean text, split, compare or merge them, and writes the results back to that same spreadsheet.

This processing happens entirely inside Google's Apps Script platform. Rinse does not send your spreadsheet contents to us or to anyone else, does not store copies outside your spreadsheet, and cannot open your other files. Before each change Rinse saves a hidden backup tab inside your spreadsheet so you can undo; you can delete it anytime.

Data we store

Using Google's per-user storage for add-ons, Rinse stores your license key (if you have one), its status, and a count of free clean-ups used today. In the spreadsheet's own storage it records which sheet the last backup belongs to.

License checks

If you activate Rinse Pro, the sidebar sends your license key to our payment reseller (Lemon Squeezy) to confirm it is valid, once when you activate and about once a day afterwards. No spreadsheet data is included. Purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record under its own privacy policy; we receive your name, email and order details from it so we can provide support and refunds.

What we do not do

We do not sell data, show ads, use analytics trackers, or use your data to train AI models. Rinse's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Uninstall Rinse from Extensions, Add-ons, Manage add-ons, and remove its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Delete any tabs named _rinse_backup_. To delete purchase records, email us.
